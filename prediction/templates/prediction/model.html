Model card

Intended useEducation and demonstration of an uncertainty-aware ML workflow. Not a medical device.
Not intended forDiagnosis, treatment decisions, triage, or use on patients.
Training dataUCI Cleveland heart disease data, {{ info.rows }} complete records, 13 attributes, target converted to disease / no disease.
Known limitsSmall, single-site, mostly older patients, historical data. Inputs outside the training range are flagged by the trust check.
ExplanationsMedian-replacement ablation: each input is swapped for the training median and the change in probability is shown. Associations, not causes.
